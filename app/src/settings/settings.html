    Add a channel

    An HLS stream (.m3u8) adds one channel. An M3U playlist adds every channel in it.

    Import

    Files can be TV Thing channel packs (.tvthing) or M3U playlists. Channels already in your lineup are skipped.

    Picture and sound

    Sound timingShift the sound if it doesn't match the picture. You can also do this on the Car Thing: press the knob twice, then turn.
    In sync

    On the Car Thing

    Knob Turn for volume. Press for the channel guide, where turning browses and pressing tunes. Press on the channel that's already playing to adjust sound timing.

    1–4 Favorite channels. In the guide, press a button to save the highlighted channel to it.

    Front button Mute, or back out of the guide.

    Screen Tap to switch between Fill, Fit and Zoom.

    Edit channel

    Automatic sends streams straight to the Car Thing when it can play them, and converts the rest with FFmpeg.
